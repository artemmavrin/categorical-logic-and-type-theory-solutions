\begin{exercise}
Define for a subset \(X \subseteq I\) the relation \(\nEq(X) \subseteq I \times I\) by
\begin{equation*}
\nEq(X) = \SetOf[\big]{\Pair{i}{i^\prime} \SuchThat \text{\(i \neq i^\prime\) or \(i \in X\)}}
\end{equation*}
and show that the assignment \(X \mapsto \nEq(X)\) is right adjoint to contraction \(\delta^* : \PowerSet{I \times I} \to \PowerSet{I}\).
Notice that \(\nEq(X)\) at the bottom element \(X = \emptyset\) is inequality on \(I\).
\end{exercise}

\begin{solution}
Fix a set \(I\) and consider the functors
\begin{equation*}
\begin{tikzcd}
\PowerSet{I} \arrow[r, shift right=0.5ex, "\nEq"{below}] & \PowerSet{I \times I}. \arrow[l, shift right=0.5ex, "\delta^*"{above}]
\end{tikzcd}
\end{equation*}
To show that \(\delta^* \dashv \nEq\), we fix \(X \subseteq I\) and \(Y \subseteq I \times I\) and claim that
\(\delta^*(Y) \subseteq X\) if and only if \(Y \subseteq \nEq(X)\).
Suppose \(\delta^*(Y) \subseteq X\) and \(\Pair{i}{j} \in Y\).
If \(i \neq j\), then \(\Pair{i}{j} \in \nEq(X)\), and if \(i = j\), then \(i \in \delta^*(Y) \subseteq X\), and hence \(\Pair{i}{j} \in \nEq(X)\).
Thus, \(Y \subseteq \nEq(X)\).
Conversely, suppose \(Y \subseteq \nEq(X)\) and \(i \in \delta^*(Y)\).
Then \(\Pair{i}{i} \in Y \subseteq \nEq(X)\), so \(i \in X\), whence \(\delta^*(Y) \subseteq X\).
\end{solution}
