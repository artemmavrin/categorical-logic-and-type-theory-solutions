\begin{exercise}
Check that for a PER \(R\) one has \(R \subseteq \Carrier*{R}^2\).
\end{exercise}

\begin{solution}
Let \(R\) be a partial equivalence relation (on an arbitrary set, not necessarily just \(\NN\)), and let \(\Pair{x}{y} \in R\).
Since \(R\) is symmetric, we also have \(\Pair{y}{x} \in R\), and since \(R\) is transitive, it follows that \(\Pair{x}{x} \in R\) and \(\Pair{y}{y} \in R\).
Thus, \(x \in \Carrier{R}\) and \(y \in \Carrier{R}\), so \(\Pair{x}{y} \in \Carrier*{R}^2\).
Therefore \(R \subseteq \Carrier*{R}^2\).
\end{solution}
